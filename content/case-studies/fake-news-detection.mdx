## The problem

Misinformation spreads across languages, and most tooling only works in English. Pakistan's media landscape mixes English and Urdu, so a useful classifier has to handle more than one language.

## What I built

A multilingual NLP system that classifies news text as **real or fake** in **English, Urdu and Spanish**, built end-to-end in Python with TensorFlow, Scikit-learn and NLP libraries.

## How it works

<Diagram />

Text is cleaned and preprocessed, then classified with two complementary approaches:

- **Multilingual BERT** (`bert-base-multilingual-cased`, loaded with Hugging Face Transformers): a transformer that understands words in context across languages.
- **An SVM:** a fast classical model on engineered text features.

Careful preprocessing and feature engineering mattered as much as the model choice.

## Results

- **90% accuracy** classifying real vs. fake news.
- Works across three languages instead of English only.
