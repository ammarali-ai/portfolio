## The problem

Before a sales call, someone has to research the lead: their business profile, competitors, what shows up in search, and what their own website says. Done by hand, it's slow and inconsistent.

## What I built

An n8n workflow that does the research the moment a lead comes in, and emails a strategy brief.

## How it works

<Diagram />

- **Intake:** a webhook receives the lead and normalizes it.
- **Research:** the workflow fans out to four lookups **in parallel**: the business profile (Google Places), competitors, search results (a SERP API) and the lead's website.
- **Analysis:** the findings are assembled into one enrichment record, and an LLM (Groq) writes a strategic analysis that is parsed, turned into a brief and emailed.

Running the lookups in parallel keeps the whole run fast, and assembling them first means the model sees all the evidence at once.

## Results

Every lead arrives with the same researched brief, whether it comes in at 2 p.m. or 2 a.m.
