## The problem

Teams that meet in Discord lose decisions and action items in the scroll. Writing up notes is a chore nobody wants, so it often doesn't happen.

## What I built

A Discord bot written in TypeScript with discord.js, plus three n8n workflows:

- **Slash commands:** `/summarize`, `/digest`, `/ask`, `/status` and `/help`.
- **Collection:** the bot gathers the conversation and parses it into a transcript.
- **Hand-off:** it sends the transcript to n8n through a webhook.
- **Prompts:** Gemini prompts for the meeting summary, action items, follow-up questions and a weekly digest, with a JSON schema for the output.

## How it works

<Diagram />

- **Main workflow:** validates the request, fetches and parses the messages, splits long meetings into chunks, and asks Gemini for **strict-JSON notes**: a short summary, key topics, decisions, and action items with owner, due date and priority.
- **Delivery:** the result becomes a Discord embed with the full notes in a thread underneath, and every meeting is logged to Google Sheets.
- **Scheduled digest:** a second workflow reads the log every morning and posts a digest of the last seven days.
- **Error handler:** a third workflow reports any failure back to Discord, so problems don't fail silently.

## Results

Meeting notes appear in the channel as soon as someone runs `/summarize`, with owners and due dates already on the action items, and a searchable log builds up in Sheets over time.
