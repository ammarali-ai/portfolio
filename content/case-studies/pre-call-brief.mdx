## The problem

Sales reps walk into calls under-prepared because researching a prospect takes time they don't have. A brief only helps if it arrives before the call, in a format people actually open.

## What I built

An n8n workflow that turns one form submission into a researched call brief, delivered as **PDF and DOCX** attachments by email.

## How it works

<Diagram />

- **Research and draft:** the form payload is normalized, and a **single Claude call** researches the prospect and drafts the brief. One well-structured call kept the workflow simple and fast.
- **QA and conversion:** the output is extracted, run through QA checks and converted to PDF and DOCX.
- **Delivery:** the brief is emailed with both attachments, and the form gets a response.

## Results

Every booked call gets a consistent, researched brief without anyone having to write it.
