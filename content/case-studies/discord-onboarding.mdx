## The problem

Adding new team members to the team's Discord server was a manual step: share an invite, check who joined, record their details. It's small, but it's error-prone and easy to forget.

## What I built

A one-click onboarding flow in n8n built on **Discord OAuth2**, plus a companion form flow that records new members in a data table.

## How it works

<Diagram />

- **Token exchange:** the OAuth2 redirect hits a webhook, and the workflow exchanges the authorisation code for an access token.
- **Join the server:** it reads the user's Discord profile and adds them to the server.
- **Errors:** each step that can fail returns a clear error response instead of a blank page.

## Results

Joining the team server became a single click for new members, with their details recorded automatically.

## What I'd do next

Assign roles automatically based on the team a new member joins.
