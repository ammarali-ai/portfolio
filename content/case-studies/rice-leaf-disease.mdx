## The problem

Rice diseases are easier to manage when they're caught early, and early signs show up on the leaves. Could an image classifier identify the disease reliably, and which kind of model generalises best on domain-specific data?

## What I built

My final-year project: an image-based classification system trained and validated on a dataset of **10,000+ rice leaf images**, processed with OpenCV. Instead of picking one model up front, I compared four approaches on the same data.

## How it works

<Diagram />

- **CNN:** a convolutional network trained from scratch with TensorFlow and Keras.
- **SVM:** a classical support vector machine on image features.
- **Transfer learning:** a pretrained network fine-tuned on the leaf images.
- **Random forest:** a tree-ensemble baseline.

## Results

- **92% accuracy** on the classification task.
- **Transfer learning generalised best** of the four approaches, a useful lesson for data-scarce domains where training from scratch overfits.
