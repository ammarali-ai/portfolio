## The problem

Cotton diseases damage yield, and like rice they show up on the leaves. The question for this semester project was whether a CNN could classify cotton leaf images into disease categories reliably.

## What I built

A convolutional neural network built with TensorFlow, with a preprocessing and **data augmentation** pipeline in OpenCV to make the most of a limited image set.

## How it works

<Diagram />

Images are cleaned and augmented (varied so the model sees more realistic conditions), then a CNN learns to classify each leaf into one of 5+ disease categories.

## Results

- **89% accuracy** across 5+ disease categories.
- Came before my final-year [Rice Leaf Disease AI](/projects/rice-leaf-disease) project, where I compared four approaches on a larger dataset.
