## The problem

A team ran its work on a task board but talked in Discord. Status changes, comments and blockers lived on the board, so people either kept checking it or missed things. Updates were posted by hand, and a blocked task could sit unnoticed.

## What I built

A production n8n system that listens to task events in real time and brings them to where the team already is:

- **New task:** the assignee gets a direct message straight away.
- **Status changes:** progress updates are posted to the team channel.
- **Comments:** everyone involved in the task is notified.
- **Blockers:** when an assignee flags a blocker, the task creator and their manager are alerted immediately.
- **Milestones:** team-wide announcements when major work lands.

## How it works

<Diagram />

Events arrive over a secure webhook. Every payload is validated first, and invalid events go to error handling instead of silently disappearing. Valid events are mapped to the right people (assignee, creator, manager) from a data table, analysed, and delivered as Discord messages.

## Results

- Status updates no longer have to be posted by hand.
- Blockers reach the right people as soon as they're flagged, not when someone next checks the board.
- The team has a single source of truth in the place it already chats.
