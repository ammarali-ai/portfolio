## The question

Crop-disease models look great on clean benchmark images. Farmers' photos are blurry, badly lit and taken on low-end phones, and labelled data is scarce. What actually works under those conditions?

## What I've built so far

- [Rice Leaf Disease AI](/projects/rice-leaf-disease): 10,000+ images, four approaches compared (CNN, SVM, transfer learning, random forest). Transfer learning generalised best, at 92% accuracy.
- [Cotton Crop Disease Detection](/projects/cotton-crop-disease): a CNN with a preprocessing and augmentation pipeline, at 89% accuracy across 5+ disease categories.

## Directions I want to explore

- Detection instead of classification: _where_ on the leaf the disease is (see my note on [YOLO](/research/yolo-realtime)).
- Synthetic data for rare diseases (see [diffusion models](/research/diffusion-models)).
- Small models that run offline on a phone.
