## The question

An LLM is great at the judgement step of a workflow (classify this call, summarise this event, decide this branch) but it is not deterministic. How do you keep a production automation predictable when one of its steps is a model?

## What I've built so far

- The [Real-Time Monitoring & Alert System](/projects/realtime-monitoring) validates every event before anything else runs and sends bad payloads to error handling instead of dropping them.
- The [Call Analyzer](/projects/call-analyzer) asks the model for a strict JSON schema and parses and validates it before anything is saved or emailed.
- The [LLM Prompt Feedback Loop](/projects/prompt-feedback-loop) feeds real outcomes back into prompt tuning every week.

## Patterns I want to write up

- Schema-first outputs: structured outputs plus validation, and what to do when validation fails.
- Retries vs. human hand-off: when to try again and when to escalate.
- Observability: logging the inputs and decisions you'll need when something goes wrong.
