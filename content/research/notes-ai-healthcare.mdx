## The question

Medical imaging and clinical text have the same core problem as my agricultural work: valuable signal, little labelled data, high cost of mistakes. What transfers, and what has to change when the stakes are higher?

## What transfers from my work

- Transfer learning on small image datasets ([Rice Leaf Disease AI](/projects/rice-leaf-disease)).
- Multilingual text classification with BERT ([Fake News Detection](/projects/fake-news-detection)).
- Grounded LLM answers that refuse to guess (the "Ask Ammar" assistant on this site).

## Directions I want to explore

- Medical image classification with limited data.
- Clinical note summarisation grounded in the source text.
- Triage assistants that know when to defer to a human.
