## The question

Finance operations run on documents (invoices, statements, receipts) and on judgement calls about risk. How much of that can be automated reliably, and how do you keep it explainable?

## Building blocks I already use

- Structured extraction with LLMs and strict JSON schemas, as in the [Call Analyzer](/projects/call-analyzer) and [CV Ranker](/projects/cv-ranker).
- n8n workflows for invoices and reporting.

## Directions I want to explore

- Invoice and document extraction with validation against business rules.
- Transaction anomaly signals that a human can audit.
- Risk summaries that cite the data they're based on.
