## The question

Security teams drown in alerts. Most are noise, and the real ones are hard to explain to non-specialists. Where can ML and LLMs genuinely help, without becoming another source of noise?

## Where I'm coming from

- Monitoring endpoint threat and compliance data on Cisco AMP dashboards in an IT operations role.
- A Digital Forensics & Cybersecurity certification (NAVTTC).
- [Fake News Detection](/projects/fake-news-detection): NLP for spotting manipulated content in three languages.

## Directions I want to explore

- Anomaly detection on endpoint and network metrics.
- LLM summaries of security alerts that say what happened, why it matters and what to do.
- Measuring false-positive cost, not just accuracy.
